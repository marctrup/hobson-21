Hobson — the AI co-worker built for property

Hobson AI is an AI co-worker for property teams. It reads your leases, licences, certificates and management agreements, builds an understanding of your portfolio, and answers questions in plain English with the evidence behind every answer. Ask a question, ask Hobson to take care of something, or leave the routine work with it.

What does Hobson AI do?

Hobson answers questions about your property documents and completes routine property work on your behalf. Every document you share becomes part of its understanding of your portfolio, so it can connect information across leases, plans, reports and records rather than searching one file at a time. Every answer links back to the clause and page it came from.

How is Hobson different from a general AI assistant?

A general assistant reads a document you paste into it. Hobson understands your business: your properties, your people, your policies and your obligations. It keeps that understanding up to date over time, so answers reflect your portfolio rather than the words in a single file. Work is prepared for you, but nothing is sent or actioned until you approve it.

How does Hobson work?

You only ever speak to Hobson. Behind it sits a team of specialist agents that Hobson coordinates quietly. Four maintain what Hobson knows — portfolio knowledge, portfolio structure, compliance position and relationships. Four provide services on demand — external research, calculations and finance, systems and integrations, and access and security.

Who is Hobson AI for?

Hobson is built for organisations where property documents matter: asset managers handling lease events and compliance, retail operators managing rent reviews and break clauses across a multi-site estate, hospitality businesses tracking site leases and certificates, and corporate occupiers where property is a material cost but not the core business.

What can Hobson prepare for you?

A typical example: Hobson flags a rent review due in 60 days, drafts the recommendation with the passing rent, proposed rent and uplift, gathers audit-trailed comparable lettings, drafts the notice and covering email, and sets the reminder schedule. Everything waits for your approval.

How much does Hobson AI cost?

Hobson uses capacity-based pricing: plans differ by the number of users, documents and seats, with monthly and annual billing and a saving for paying annually. Enterprise arrangements are available for larger portfolios. Full details are on the pricing page.
